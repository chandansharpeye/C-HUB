90%+ Exam Strategy

No plan can promise marks, but these habits stack the odds in your favour. Follow them in this order.

📅 Save your exam dates in Exams & Reminders.

Know the paperStudy the syllabus, marking scheme and weightage first. Spend most time on high-weightage chapters.
Textbook firstFinish the main textbook and its exercises before any guide or extra material.
Active recallClose the book and write or say what you remember. Re-reading feels easy but teaches less.
Spaced revisionRevise on day 1, 3, 7 and 21. Short and frequent beats one long cram.
Past papersSolve the last 5 to 10 years' papers in exam time. Check with the marking scheme, not just the answer.
Error notebookWrite each mistake with the correct method. Redo it after 3 days until it never repeats.
Write for marksUse headings, key terms, steps and neat diagrams. Marks are given for steps, not only the final answer.
Exam dayUse the first 10 minutes to read and plan. Start with your strongest questions, watch time per mark, and keep 10 minutes to check.
Body and mindSleep 7 hours, eat on time and take a short walk. A rested brain remembers more.

Commerce Corner

Key ideas from Accountancy, Business Studies and Economics at a glance.

Golden rules of accounting

  • Personal: debit the receiver, credit the giver.
  • Real: debit what comes in, credit what goes out.
  • Nominal: debit expenses and losses, credit incomes and gains.

Three subjects, three questions

  • Accountancy: how do we record money?
  • Business Studies: how is a business run?
  • Economics: how do choices shape markets?

    ❓ FAQs

    Quick answers about C-HUB.

    Is C-HUB free? Do I need to log in?

    C-HUB is free and has no login or account. Open it and start using the tools.

    Where is my data saved?

    Notes, goals, marks, exams, flashcards and other data are saved in your own browser (localStorage) on this device. Clearing browser data or changing phone removes it, so use Export in My Study Tools → Settings to keep a backup and Import to restore it.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. PDF and image tools run on your device. Everything runs on your device and your files are not uploaded anywhere.

    Why do the PDF tools need internet the first time?

    They load a small open-source PDF library (pdf.js or pdf-lib) from a public CDN. Your files are not sent there; only the library is downloaded.

    Why does a PDF say it is locked or could not be read?

    Password-protected, damaged or unsupported PDFs cannot be opened. Remove the password and try again.

    Is compressed PDF text selectable?

    No. Compress PDF turns pages into images to reduce size, so use it for scanned or photo PDFs.

    Will the resized photo be accepted on every exam form?

    Not always. Each website has its own rules for size, pixels and format. Use the preset or custom size and check the website rules before uploading.

    Are the auto-made notes always correct?

    No. Notes are made from the text you give. Always check them with your textbook or teacher.

    Is C-HUB a government website?

    No. C-HUB is an independent student website. The Government Links section only points to official portals such as UIDAI, NSP, SAMS and the GST portal.

    Can I use C-HUB offline?

    Many tools work offline once the page is loaded. PDF tools need internet the first time to load their library.

    How do I install it as an app?

    Open Menu → Install as App. On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    How can I contact you or suggest a feature?

    Join our WhatsApp Channel (link in the footer and the green button) for updates and announcements.

    🔒 Privacy Policy

    Last updated: October 2026

    C-HUB is a free student website. We keep it simple: no login, no account, no database and no tracking of your files.

    What stays on your device

    Everything you type or save (notes, marks, exams, homework, attendance, flashcards, resume, settings) is stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device. Files you choose for PDF, image and notes tools are processed on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

    What we do not collect

    We do not ask for your name, email or phone to use the tools, and we do not sell or share your data. The optional name in Settings stays on your device.

    Third-party services

    • Fonts and open-source PDF libraries load from public CDNs (Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr). Those services may see your IP address as with any website. Your files are not sent to them.
    • Links like WhatsApp, Claude or ChatGPT open only when you tap them, and the text you choose to send goes to that service under its own policy.

    Your control

    You can export, import or delete all saved data from My Study Tools → Settings, or by clearing your browser data.

    Children

    C-HUB is made for students. Younger students should use it with a parent or teacher's guidance.
